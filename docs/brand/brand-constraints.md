# Brand Constraints

Yellow Music United is being **redesigned**, not rebranded. Future visual and UX work must preserve the existing identity while elevating perceived quality and professionalism.

## Preserve (non-negotiable)

- **Existing logo** — do not replace or substantially redraw without explicit product decision
- **Yellow identity** — yellow remains a core brand signal, not a generic accent
- **Existing photographic world** — authentic studio, people, and creative environments from the current site
- **Sunflower imagery** where appropriate — part of the warm, human brand story (e.g. founder portrait in sunflower field)
- **Warm, creative personality** — positivity, safety, courage, community
- **Personal / human character** — Biim and the team as real guides, not a faceless agency

## Target perception

> Clearly Yellow Music, but professionally art-directed and substantially more established.

The site should feel like a serious creative partner in Uppsala — welcoming to beginners and experienced artists alike — without losing warmth.

## May change substantially

- Layout and grid systems
- Typography (selection and hierarchy — not brand erasure)
- Spacing and rhythm
- Navigation and information architecture
- Image presentation (cropping, art direction, sequencing)
- Page composition and section hierarchy
- Responsiveness and interaction design
- Motion and micro-interactions (tasteful, not gimmicky)

## Avoid

- Generic SaaS / startup template appearance
- Excessive glassmorphism or trendy UI chrome
- Generic Wix/template aesthetic (even if the current site is Wix-hosted)
- Generic stock photography unrelated to Yellow Music
- Excessive card grids with no narrative purpose
- Losing or diluting the yellow identity
- Cold, corporate music-industry clichés

## Voice & tone (content)

Derived from existing copy — maintain in future content work:

- Swedish primary audience; professional but approachable
- Emphasis on **vägledning**, **trygghet**, **mod**, **community**
- Navigator metaphor: helping people take the next step in their creative journey
- Inclusive, gender-equality aware positioning (founder story)
